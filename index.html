CRAFT & FORM
How a Project Moves

Three stages, one team.

Every project — regardless of scale — moves through the same three stages, with the same people at the table throughout.

01 / Site & Brief

Reading the ground first.

We start on site — surveying light, sound, and how the place already wants to be used — alongside a working brief with the client.

02 / Design Development

Plan, section, and material together.

Layout, structure, and finishes are tested in the same weekly sessions, with the client in the room, not reviewing after the fact.

03 / Construction to Handover

We stay through the build.

Our team is on site through construction, resolving details in real time rather than leaving them open to interpretation.
